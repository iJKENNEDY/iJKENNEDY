Engineering Philosophy

How I approach building software.

Maintainability

Code should be easy to read, change, and delete. I write for the next engineer, not just the compiler.

Simplicity

The best architecture is the simplest one that solves the problem. Complexity is a cost, not a feature.

Scalability

Design for growth from day one. Decouple components, define clear interfaces, and plan for what changes.

Observability

If you can't measure it, you can't improve it. Logging, metrics, and tracing are first-class citizens.

Product Thinking

Engineering exists to serve users. Every technical decision should trace back to a real problem worth solving.
